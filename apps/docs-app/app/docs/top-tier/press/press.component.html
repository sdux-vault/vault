Usage Guidelines
↑ top

Permitted

  • Use the wordmark or brand variant when referencing or by name in articles, blog posts, or presentations.
  • Use the symbol or logo variant as an icon in toolbars, dashboards, or integration listings.
  • Maintain the original aspect ratio and do not alter colors or proportions.

Not Permitted

  • Do not use logos in a way that implies endorsement or affiliation without permission.
  • Do not modify, distort, or recolor the logos.
  • Do not use logos as part of your own product name or branding.

For full trademark policy details, see the Trademark Usage Policy.
