Timeline — Category Duration Spans

The category duration spans timeline shows how long each event category took from its first to its last event. Each colored bar represents the time window a category was active during the trace.

Compare the width and position of spans between before and after traces to see which categories grew, shrank, or shifted in timing. A wider span means the category took longer; a shifted span means it started or ended at a different point.

Use zoom to magnify the timeline and reveal finer detail. Tick lines adapt to the zoom level.
